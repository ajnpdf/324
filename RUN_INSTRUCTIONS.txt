AJN PDF Multiplatform V1

1. Download and extract this ZIP.
2. Copy both .ps1 files into:
   C:\Users\ANJAN PATEL\Downloads\AJN-PDF-dev (2)\AJN-PDF-dev

3. Open PowerShell in that AJN-PDF folder.
4. Run:

Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass -Force; & ".\SETUP_AJN_PDF_MULTIPLATFORM_V1.ps1"

The script creates and pushes:
production/multiplatform-v1

After external IDs/signing are configured, build releases with:

Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass -Force; & ".\BUILD_AJN_PDF_RELEASES.ps1"

Production ads example:

Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass -Force; & ".\BUILD_AJN_PDF_RELEASES.ps1" `
  -ProductionAds `
  -AdMobBannerAndroid "YOUR_BANNER_ID" `
  -AdMobInterstitialAndroid "YOUR_INTERSTITIAL_ID"


Before building the Play AAB, configure signing once:

Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass -Force; & ".\CONFIGURE_AJN_ANDROID_SIGNING.ps1"
